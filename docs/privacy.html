Privacy Policy – Movie Splitter

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Movie Splitter is a personal tool used by one person (the owner) to cut their own videos into short clips and post them to their own TikTok account.

What data is used

When the owner connects TikTok, the tool receives the basic profile information TikTok provides (such as display name) and permission to upload videos to that account. It is used only to post the owner's own clips.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. Login tokens are stored by Composio (the connection service) and on the owner's own computer.

Other users

The tool is not offered to the public and collects no data about other people.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time in TikTok settings or in Composio, which removes access.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/Mmegar/movie-splitter-v2.
