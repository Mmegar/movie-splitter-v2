# Background music

`tracks.json` lists free-to-use songs. The GitHub workflow downloads them before cutting (they aren't
stored in this public repo), and each part's caption gets the credit line the song's licence asks for.

- **calm** songs play quietly under the narrator in Movie recap and Character story (`music_volume`).
- **hype** songs drive the Hype edit: the montage is cut to the song's beat, starting at its loudest stretch.

The starting list is Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed CC BY 4.0: free for TikTok as long as the
credit is in the caption, which the cutter does for you.

**Adding your own:** drop an `.mp3` (or `.m4a`, `.wav`) into this folder and commit it, or add an entry to
`tracks.json` with a direct download link. Only use songs you're allowed to post (your own, YouTube Audio
Library, Pixabay Music...). Trending TikTok sounds are copyrighted, so they can't be baked into the video;
to use one, turn `music` off in Settings and add the sound in TikTok when you post.
